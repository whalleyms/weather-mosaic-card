Weather Mosaic Card — live preview

A Home Assistant dashboard card that shows a week of hourly weather as a color grid — or a spiral. Enter a city anywhere in the world, or use your location, and see your own next 7 days rendered with a real forecast.

GitHub Documentation Download HACS
Forecast and geocoding by Open-Meteo (free, no account). Your search term or location is sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast; nothing is stored here. Open-Meteo may differ slightly from whatever weather integration you run in Home Assistant.
